About Me

I am a data enthusiast with a particular passion for complex networks and systems. I am a postdoc in the COSS research group at ETH Zurich and lecturer in the Data Science in Techno-Socio-Economic Systems course.

I co-founded aequa-tech, a Turin-based spin-off developing NLP, machine learning, and complex networks tools with a focus on fairness and digital disparity. My work spans data science, network analysis, graph neural networks, NLP, and data visualization.
